Write the funniest one-line fortune cookie message for a software engineer,
1–120 characters, judged by an AI comedy editor pairwise against other entries.

Judge criteria, most important first:
1. A real fortune cookie message for a software engineer (not a general slogan).
2. Genuinely funny on first read, with a real punchline — not a pun that only half works.
3. Sounds like a fortune cookie: short, declarative, twist from the engineer's world of code, deploys, bugs and standups.
4. Fresh: a line nobody has seen on a mug, a sticker or a conference slide.
5. Tight: every word earns its place, funniest word at the end.

Strong direction: a fortune that disguises career advice but is really about a
bug, prod, or a Friday-afternoon deploy — specific, not generic wordplay like
"you will meet a tall dark stack trace".

Deliverable: main.py (stdlib only) that deterministically writes the single
final line to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt (default /output/entry.txt),
plus README.md (3 make.fun badges, the final text quoted, why it wins),
CANDIDATES.md (10 candidates with reasons, why the winner was chosen),
LICENSE (MIT + SPDX line), .makefun/owner.json, .gitignore.
Verify: run main.py 3x (byte-identical sha256), clean-venv run, --check mode.
